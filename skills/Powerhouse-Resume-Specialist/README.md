# Powerhouse Resume Specialist

A premium **Career Document Specialist** designed to generate and format pixel-perfect, ATS-optimized resumes in `.docx` format.

> [!CAUTION]
> AI-generated Word documents often have broken margins and unreadable tables. This skill enforces a **strict layout blueprint** to ensure your resume looks professional to both humans and scanners.

---

## 🎨 Professional Standard

*   **Pixel-Perfect Margins**: Hard-coded 0.5" - 0.6" margins (US Letter standard).
*   **Tab-Stop Alignment**: Uses professional right-aligned tab stops for dates (no more "spacebar" alignment).
*   **ATS-Friendly**: Zero tables, zero images—pure semantic DOCX structure that recruiters' systems can parse perfectly.
*   **Premium Palette**: Elegant Navy/Steel Blue color scheme with Calibri-driven typography.

---

## 🛠️ Installation

1.  Download [Powerhouse-Resume-Specialist.skill](./Powerhouse-Resume-Specialist.skill)
2.  Open [Claude.ai](https://claude.ai) -> **Settings** -> **Skills**.
3.  Click **Install Skill** and upload the file.
